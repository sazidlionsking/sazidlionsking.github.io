What is Sargam Player?

Sargam Player is a free, ad-free offline music player for Android. Download the Sargam Player APK and play the songs and videos already stored on your phone, with no internet needed. It has a clean design that takes its colours from your songs, playlists, a reorderable queue, an equalizer with bass boost, a sleep timer, playback speed, lyrics support, background play and a home-screen widget. If you are looking for a simple, fast and good-looking music player app for Android that works without internet and has no ads, Sargam Player is made for you.

Questions

What is the Sargam Player APK?

The APK is the installation file of Sargam Player, a free offline music player for Android. Download it from this page and install it on your phone.

Is it free? Are there ads?

Yes, it is free, and there are no ads at all. There is no account to create either.

Does it come with songs?

No. Sargam Player plays the music and videos that are already on your phone. It does not download or stream songs.

Why does Android show a "Play Protect" message?

Because the app is installed outside the Play Store and is new to Google. Tap "Scan app" or "Install anyway". The app only reads the media you give it access to.

How do I update?

The app tells you when a new version is ready. Tap "Update now", download the new APK and install it over the old one. Your playlists stay.

Will music keep playing when I close the app?

Yes. Sargam keeps playing in the background with controls in the notification. Close the notification to stop it. On some phones, set battery to "No restrictions" for Sargam so the system does not stop it.

Which Android versions work?

Sargam Player is built for modern Android phones. If it does not install on yours, please let us know.
